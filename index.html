A few everyday clues to help you identify your natural undertone:

  • Vein color: If your wrist veins appear green or olive in natural daylight → Warm. If they appear blue or violet → Cool. If you see a subtle mix of both → Neutral.
  • Jewelry shortcut: If yellow gold brings out warmth in your face → Warm. If pure silver illuminates your features → Cool. If you wear both effortlessly → Neutral.
  • White fabric test: If warm ivory feels softer near your face than bright optic white, you likely lean warm.

* This is only a styling shortcut, not a scientific test. Wear what makes you feel happiest and most confident!

        Behind the Guide

        How StyleSense Works

        1. Input & Gentle Proportions: You enter your age, height, weight, and styling preference. Your BMI is computed strictly in your browser to help suggest breathable, comfortable silhouette lines. We explicitly provide non-judgmental guidance and separate handling for users under 18.

        2. Skin Tone & Undertone Harmony: Selecting your skin depth (from Fair to Deep across seven inclusive South Asian & global tones) and undertone (Warm, Cool, or Neutral) unlocks color theory harmonies grounded in natural contrast.

        3. Rule-Based Local Recommendations: Our comprehensive local dataset (data.js) instantly assembles your tailored color palette, Indian ethnic and contemporary outfits, jewelry metals, bags, footwear, and hairstyles.

        4. Total Privacy Guarantee: No logins, no tracking, no servers, and no external AI calls. Your data never leaves your device.
